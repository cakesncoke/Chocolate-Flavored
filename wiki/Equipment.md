# Equipment

Chocolate Flavored reworks vanilla tools, weapons and armor and adds a few new ones.

## Removed items

These items can no longer be crafted, found in chests, traded for or seen in the creative inventory:

- Wooden shovel, pickaxe, axe, hoe and spear
- Stone sword, shovel, pickaxe, hoe and spear
- Copper spear, helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots, horse armor and nautilus armor

The wooden sword and the stone axe remain, but are much more fragile (see below).

## Iron is now Steel

All iron equipment (tools, spear, armor, horse armor and nautilus armor) is called **Steel** equipment. Its stats are those of the former iron equipment, with the changes below.

## Durability and mining speed

| Item | Change |
|---|---|
| Armor (leather, chainmail, steel, diamond, netherite, turtle shell) | durability ×2 (gold armor unchanged) |
| Copper and steel tools and weapons | durability ×2.5 (copper 475, steel 625) |
| Diamond and netherite tools and weapons | durability ×2 (3122 / 4062) |
| Diamond and netherite tools | mining speed +1 (9 / 10) |
| Trident, fishing rod | durability ×3 (750 / 192) |
| Bow, crossbow | durability ×1.5 (576 / 698) |
| Wooden sword | durability 16 |
| Stone axe | durability 32 |

**Hoes** now hit harder: a hoe deals 0.5 less damage than the pickaxe of the same material (for example a steel hoe deals 3.5 damage, a steel pickaxe 4).

**Gold tools and weapons have an innate Silk Touch**, shown in their tooltip.

## Rose Gold tools

Sword, shovel, pickaxe, axe and hoe. They match steel tools, except:

| Stat | Rose gold |
|---|---|
| Durability | 975 (steel's 625 + 350) |
| Mining speed | 7 |
| Enchantability | 18 |

They are repaired with rose gold ingots. A copper tool becomes its rose gold counterpart at a smithing table with a **rose gold upgrade smithing template** and a **rose gold ingot**. Rose gold ingots can currently only be crafted from rose gold nuggets or blocks; there is no way to obtain them in survival yet.

## Hammers

| | Durability | Mining speed | Attack damage | Attack speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stone Hammer | 131 | 3 | 10 | 0.3 |
| Heavy Hammer | 190 | 5 | 10 | 0.5 |

See [Stone and Materials](Stone_and_Materials.md) for what hammers do to blocks.

## Knives

| | Durability | Attack damage | Attack speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flint Knife | 32 | 2.5 | 2 |
| Iron Knife | 250 | 3.5 | 2 |

Knives gather **plant fiber** from plants and make leaves drop sticks more often. See [Stone and Materials](Stone_and_Materials.md).

## Recipes

| Item | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Flint Knife | flint above a stick |
| Iron Knife | iron ingot above a stick |
| Stone Hammer | 5 cobblestone-type blocks (cobblestone, blackstone, cobbled deepslate) and 2 sticks, in the shape of a T-hammer |
| Heavy Hammer | 5 iron ingots and 2 sticks, same shape |
| Rose gold tools | smithing table: copper tool + rose gold ingot + rose gold upgrade smithing template |
| Steel tools and weapons | the usual vanilla shapes using **steel ingots** instead of iron |
| Diamond tools and weapons | the usual vanilla shapes using **diamond ingots** instead of diamonds |

**Gold equipment can no longer be crafted** (tools, weapons and armor).

## Armor trims

Steel ingots and rose gold ingots can be used as trim materials at a smithing table. Steel trims on steel (former iron) armor use a darker shade, like iron trims did.

## Beacons

Diamond ingots, steel ingots and rose gold ingots can be used as beacon payment. Steel blocks and rose gold blocks can be used as beacon bases.
