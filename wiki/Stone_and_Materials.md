# Stone and Materials

## Hammers

Hammers mine stone-type blocks. Some blocks are **converted** to the next stage instead of breaking, and drop a pebble. Hammers do not convert blocks in Creative mode.

### Stone Hammer

Mines these blocks at mining speed 3, breaking them normally (stairs, slabs, walls and chiseled/polished variants included):

- Cobblestone, mossy cobblestone, cobbled granite/diorite/andesite and cobbled deepslate families
- Stone, smooth stone, stone bricks and mossy stone bricks families
- Granite, diorite, andesite, tuff, bricks, packed earth / earthen bricks, resin bricks, sandstone, red sandstone, cinnabar and sulfur families
- Calcite, gravel and the gravelly stones (granite, diorite, andesite)

It mines **deepslate and cobbled deepslate** a little more slowly (speed 2).

| Mined block | Becomes | Drops |
|---|---|---|
| Stone | Cobblestone | Stone pebble |
| Granite / Diorite / Andesite | Cobbled granite / diorite / andesite | Matching pebble |
| Cobblestone | Gravel | Stone pebble |
| Cobbled granite / diorite / andesite | Gravelly granite / diorite / andesite | Matching pebble |
| Deepslate | Cobbled deepslate | Deepslate pebble |
| Cobbled deepslate | Gravelly deepslate | Deepslate pebble |
| Coal ore | Cobblestone | 3 coal dust |
| Copper ore | Cobblestone | Copper ore pile |
| Sandstone / Red sandstone | Sand / Red sand | nothing |
| Stone bricks | Cracked stone bricks | nothing |

### Heavy Hammer

Mines everything the stone hammer does, plus **any block a pickaxe can mine**, at mining speed 5. It harvests blocks up to the stone tier, like a stone-tier pickaxe, so ores such as diamond still need a better tool. The ore rules below are the exception.

| Mined block | Becomes | Drops |
|---|---|---|
| Basalt | Cobbled basalt | Basalt pebble |
| Cobbled basalt | Gravelly basalt | Basalt pebble |
| Iron ore | Cobblestone | Iron ore pile |
| Gold ore | Cobblestone | Gold ore pile |
| Lapis ore | Cobblestone | 2-6 lapis lazuli |
| Deepslate coal ore | Cobbled deepslate | 3 coal dust |
| Deepslate copper / iron / gold ore | Cobbled deepslate | Copper / iron / gold ore pile |
| Deepslate lapis ore | Cobbled deepslate | 2-6 lapis lazuli |
| Deepslate tiles / bricks | Cracked version | nothing |
| Polished blackstone bricks | Cracked polished blackstone bricks | nothing |
| Nether bricks | Cracked nether bricks | nothing |

## Knives

- Breaking **short grass, fern, bush or red shrub** drops 1 plant fiber.
- Breaking **tall grass or large fern** drops 2 plant fiber.
- A knife held in the crafting grid with **plant fiber** makes **2 plant string**. The knife loses 1 durability instead of being used up.
- Breaking **leaves** gives a 33% chance of dropping sticks.

## New blocks

| Block | Notes |
|---|---|
| Cobbled Granite / Diorite / Andesite / Basalt | Mined with a pickaxe. Each has stairs, slabs and walls, crafted like their cobblestone versions or made at a stonecutter. |
| Gravelly Granite / Diorite / Andesite / Basalt / Deepslate | Mined with a shovel or hammer. |
| Steel Block | Needs a stone pickaxe tier (copper or better). |
| Rose Gold Block | Needs an iron (steel) pickaxe tier or better. |

## Gravel and flint

Gravel and all gravelly stones drop **flint 50% of the time when broken without a shovel** (by hand, with a pickaxe or with a hammer), and otherwise drop themselves. Broken with a shovel, or with any Silk Touch tool, they never drop flint.

## New materials

Pebbles (stone, andesite, diorite, granite, basalt, deepslate), coal dust, copper/iron/gold ore piles, plant fiber, iron bloom, steel ingot/nugget/plate, rose gold ingot/nugget, diamond ingot/plate, creeper oysters and a rose gold upgrade smithing template. Steel and rose gold ingots, nuggets and blocks convert between each other.

| Item | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Cobblestone, cobbled granite / diorite / andesite / basalt / deepslate | 4 of the matching pebble in a 2×2 square |
| Raw copper / iron / gold | 2 of the matching ore pile |
| Coal | 4 coal dust |
| Planks | any axe + a log of the matching wood. The axe loses 1 durability instead of being used up. Logs can no longer be crafted into planks by hand |
| Rose gold ingot | 1 gold ingot + 4 copper ingots |
| Ingots from nuggets | 4 nuggets in a 2×2 square (iron, gold, copper, steel, rose gold). An ingot now makes 4 nuggets |
| Iron bloom | 1 iron ingot surrounded by 8 coal or charcoal |
| Diamond ingot | 1 diamond, 1 iron ingot and 1 creeper oyster |

Plant string is used in place of string in the stone hammer and stone axe recipes. Plant fiber, creeper oysters, steel plates and diamond plates do not have other uses yet.
