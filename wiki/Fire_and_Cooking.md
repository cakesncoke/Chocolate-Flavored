# Fire and Cooking

## Campfires

A **campfire** is placed **unlit** (soul campfires are still placed lit). Light it with a fire starter, flint and steel, a fire charge or any other vanilla way.

**Crafting:** top row 2 sticks, bottom row 2 logs (any log). The old recipe (sticks, coal and logs) no longer works.

## Fire Starter

A primitive tool with **2 uses**. Hold use on an unlit campfire (normal or soul) or an unlit Earthen Kiln for **5 seconds** to light it. While you hold it, your hand rubs back and forth. Letting go early, looking away, moving out of reach or switching items cancels it without using up durability.

**Crafting:** a stick and a plant string on each diagonal (stick top-left and bottom-right, plant string top-right and bottom-left).

## Packed Earth and Earthen Bricks

Packed mud is called **Packed Earth** and mud bricks are called **Earthen Bricks**.

- **Packed earth:** 1 mud + 1 wheat or plant fiber, **or** 1 dirt + 3 wheat or plant fiber in a 2×2 square (dirt top-left, the rest any mix of wheat and plant fiber).
- **Earthen bricks:** cook packed earth on a campfire (30 seconds). They can no longer be crafted from packed earth.

## Earthen Kiln

A campfire-like block that cooks up to **six items** at once.

**Crafting:** a ring of 7 earthen bricks (the top row, both sides and the bottom row's corners) with a **campfire** in the bottom middle and the center empty.

- Place food on it with the item in hand, then light it with a fire starter, flint and steel or a fire charge. A shovel or a water bucket puts it out.
- It cooks everything a campfire cooks, including packed earth into earthen bricks.
- It also cooks in batches: **3 wheat make 1 bread**, and potatoes bake faster than on a campfire.
- Nothing solid may sit on top of it. If you cover a kiln, the items pop out.
- A lit kiln gives off light and burns anyone who walks on it, like a campfire.
- It smelts primitive metals (20 seconds each): **raw copper** makes 1 copper ingot, **raw iron** makes 1 iron ingot, **copper ore piles** make 2 copper nuggets each and **iron ore piles** make 2 iron nuggets each.
