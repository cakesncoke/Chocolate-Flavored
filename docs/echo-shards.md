# Lodestone Echo Shards

Right-click an Overworld lodestone with a vanilla Echo Shard to link one shard. A stack splits one linked shard into the inventory (or drops it if the inventory is full), following vanilla compass linking. Multiple shards share the same lodestone. Relinking replaces a shard’s old target. Linking is not available outside the Overworld. No new item, recipe or external dependency is added.

A linked shard glints. Hold right-click for 60 simulation ticks (three seconds at 20 TPS) to charge. Releasing early cancels without consuming it. Completing the charge breaks exactly one shard, including in creative mode. In the Overworld, it teleports the player to the top of the lodestone and plays the vanilla chorus-fruit teleport sound. It prefers a collision-free position above the lodestone, then supported spaces within two blocks if obstructed. If no space is available, the shard still breaks without teleporting. Velocity and fall distance reset on arrival. In the Nether, End or another dimension the full charge completes and the shard breaks, without teleporting or playing the teleport sound.

Links use a saved incarnation ID per placed lodestone. Block removal (including mining, explosions, commands and piston movement away from the original location) invalidates that incarnation. Every linked shard immediately loses its effective link, even in another dimension, a closed chest, nested storage, dropped items or offline inventories. A server-authorized ID cache updates visible glint without finding and rewriting every item stack. Stale link metadata is cleared when an item next ticks in an inventory or is used; it cannot activate. Replacing a lodestone at the same position never restores old links. If a lodestone breaks during a charge, the action is cancelled without consuming the now-unlinked shard.

Saved links and remaining valid incarnations survive normal world/server restarts. Unloaded destination chunks are loaded only when needed for a completed Overworld use. Charge timing follows normal simulation speed, including accelerated sleep. No seasonal mechanics are changed.
